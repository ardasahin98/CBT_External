The aim of this test is to gather responses from researchers to assess cyclic behavior type based on 16 cyclic test results.

Informed Consent

We are conducting a research study to understand the variation in cyclic behavior assessments among professionals. Participation in this survey is voluntary. We do not anticipate that the survey will pose any risks or discomfort. Benefits of participation include contributing to a better understanding of uncertainties in determining cyclic behavior from cyclic laboratory test results.

Your email address will be stored to identify unique responses, but no personally identifying information will be shared or published. You will not receive compensation for your participation. You may withdraw from the study at any time by requesting the deletion of your responses, even if the survey is only partially completed.

If you have any questions about the survey, please contact: admin@nextgenerationliquefaction.org. By entering your email address and proceeding with the survey, you acknowledge that you understand this information and voluntarily agree to participate.

The principal investigator of this study is Scott J. Brandenberg, Professor, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, UCLA.
